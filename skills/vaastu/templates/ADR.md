# ADR: Decision title

Use for an architecturally significant decision; reuse the project's decision-log convention. A routine local implementation choice normally needs no record. Suggested location when none exists: docs/architecture/decisions/0001-decision-title.md.

- Status: proposed / accepted / rejected / superseded
- Date and source context:
- Decision owner and actual review outcome:
- Related requirement, feature, or incident:
- Supersedes / superseded by:

## Context and decision drivers

What problem, constraints, evidence, and measurable requirements drive the choice? What assumptions remain unverified?

## Alternatives

Compare viable options, including keeping the current design. Explain relevant trade-offs and why options were rejected.

## Decision and rationale

State the chosen boundary or approach and why it meets the requirements. Do not invent approval or claim a proposed decision has been accepted.

## Consequences and migration

Benefits, costs, risks, affected public contracts/data/deployments, compatibility and any migration/recovery work.

## Verification and revisit conditions

Evidence required or observed, and specific changes or measurements that would invalidate the assumptions. Preserve accepted history: record a changed decision in a new ADR and link the superseding record.
